# Authentication and forms

1. Verify every field has a persistent programmatic label, instructions, autocomplete, and correct
   error association.
2. Exercise required, invalid, correction, review, and submission states.
3. Confirm authentication does not require a cognitive-function test without an allowed alternative.
4. Verify redundant entry is avoided and irreversible submissions provide confirmation or reversal.
5. Treat MFA/consent as an owner action; never capture or store credentials.
6. Exercise empty, invalid, corrected, and submitted states on each applicable form, including error
   association, announcement, focus, and recovery.

Record field semantics, errors, focus behavior, screenshots, and the tested recovery path.
