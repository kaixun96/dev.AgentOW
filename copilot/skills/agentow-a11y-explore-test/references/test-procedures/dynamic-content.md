# Dynamic content and predictable behavior

1. Exercise dialogs, menus, async loading, validation, notifications, and live updates.
2. Verify focus placement/restoration and that input/focus alone does not trigger unexpected context
   changes.
3. Confirm success, error, progress, and warning messages are exposed without moving focus.
4. Repeat updates to detect duplicate or stale announcements.
5. Record at least one executed transition for each applicable dynamic pattern; a list of expandable
   elements or live regions alone is insufficient.

Capture trigger, resulting state, focus state, live-region facts, screenshot, and exact announcement
when real AT is used.
