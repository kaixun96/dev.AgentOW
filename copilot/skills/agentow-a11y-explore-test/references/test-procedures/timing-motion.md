# Timing and motion

1. Identify time limits, auto-updating content, animation, flashing, carousels, and moving regions.
2. Verify pause/stop/hide or extension controls and that focus does not move unexpectedly.
3. Respect reduced-motion settings and verify essential status remains available.
4. Confirm no content flashes above allowed thresholds.

Record timing, user controls, reduced-motion state, and before/after evidence.
