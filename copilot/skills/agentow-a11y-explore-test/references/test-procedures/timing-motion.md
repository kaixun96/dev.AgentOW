# Timing and motion

1. Identify time limits, auto-updating content, animation, flashing, carousels, and moving regions.
2. Verify pause/stop/hide or extension controls and that focus does not move unexpectedly.
3. Test WCAG 2.2 SC 2.2.2 in ordinary motion mode: observe candidates for more than five seconds and
   capture at least two samples, and exercise the actual pause, stop, or hide mechanism.
4. Test reduced-motion separately as a supporting compatibility mode; do not use it to replace or
   determine the WCAG 2.2 SC 2.2.2 ordinary-mode result.
5. Confirm no content flashes above allowed thresholds.

Record timing, user controls, reduced-motion state, executed live steps, ordinary-mode duration and
sample count, reduced-motion duration and sample count, and before/after evidence.
